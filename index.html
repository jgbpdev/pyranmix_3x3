How to use the solver

  1. 1. Paint

    Pick a colour and click the stickers on the 3D pyramid or the flat view, until they match your puzzle. Use ↶ ↷ ⇅ or drag to see the other faces.

  2. 2. Solve

    Press Solve! The solver searches every position and returns the shortest solution: tip turns, then at most 11 layer turns.

  3. 3. Follow

    Play the solution or step forward and back. Each step has a picture. The reverse sequence rebuilds the scramble from solved.

Beginner method (layer by layer)

Step 1 · Tips

Turn each of the 4 tips (u, l, r, b) so its colours match the centre piece just below it. Every tip takes at most one turn.

Step 1b · Centres

Choose a bottom colour. Turn the L, R and B layers so the three centres around the bottom face all show that colour.

Step 2 · First layer

Insert the 3 bottom edges with the triggers R U R' and L' U' L. See the first-layer algorithms.

Step 3 · Last layer

Only 3 edges are left. Match your case to one of the 11 last-layer cases and do its algorithm.

All algorithms – forward & reverse

Each card shows the forward algorithm and its reverse (inverse). Each picture shows the case that sequence solves, as a 3D view and a flat net. Press ▶ to watch it on the big pyramid.
